# Board brief: TicketFlow (working name)

## The idea
A mobile-first SaaS for small roll-off / hauling companies (5–20 trucks) that solves the "ticket-to-invoice" problem.

How it works:
1. The driver tips the container at the disposal site (landfill / transfer station) and gets a scale ticket.
2. The driver photographs the ticket in the app.
3. AI/OCR reads the tonnage and disposal fees from the photo.
4. The system compares the actual tonnage/fees against what the customer was quoted and auto-flags any overage (e.g. weight over the included allowance).
5. A complete, invoice-ready job record is generated immediately.
6. The owner sends a Stripe payment link to the customer with one tap.

The pain it replaces: the owner spends every Friday manually reconciling handwritten scale tickets against jobs, chasing drivers for missing paperwork, and (by the founder's account) losing money on overages that are never billed.

## Customer
Owners of small roll-off / hauling companies with 5–20 trucks.

## Product and price
- Subscription: £79–£149/month, self-serve, 14-day free trial.
- Pre-build validation offer: a £99 pilot.
- MVP scope (narrow): job intake, driver assignment, photo capture, OCR extraction, overage flagging, one-tap invoicing.
- Later: driver messaging, multi-yard support, accounting integrations.

## Go-to-market
- Validate demand first: 20 operator conversations and a £99 pilot offer before writing serious code.
- Distribution: SEO and roll-off operator communities. No personal / outbound sales.

## Build and costs
- Stack: Next.js + Supabase + Stripe + Vercel.
- Running cost: under £50/month.

## Goal
25–50 paying customers within 6–12 months, reaching about £5k/month recurring revenue.
