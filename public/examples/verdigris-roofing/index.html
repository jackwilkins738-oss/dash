What “done properly” means

Five layers. Every one of them new.

A re-roof isn’t new tiles on top of old problems. Keep scrolling and we’ll take one apart.

  1. Rafters

    Checked end to end first. Anything soft or split is treated or strengthened before a single layer goes on top.

  2. Breathable membrane

    Replaces old bitumen felt: keeps rain out while letting the loft breathe, so condensation doesn’t rot the timbers.

  3. Battens

    New treated, graded timber, set to the exact gauge the tile needs — the spacing that stops water tracking back.

  4. Tiles

    Every tile mechanically fixed to the current wind-uplift standard, BS 5534 — not just hung on its nibs.

  5. Ridge

    Dry-fixed with clips and seals, so there’s no mortar left to crack and wash out in ten years.
