Four jobs. Done properly, every time.

We don’t do everything under the sun — just roofing, and the jobs that go with it, done to one standard. Here’s exactly what’s included in each.

01

Re-roofing

A full strip and re-cover when patching stops being worth it — new felt or membrane, new battens, and tiling or slating back to match (or upgrade) what was there.

Typical price
£6,500 – £18,500
On site
3–5 days
Guarantee
25 years, written
  • ✓ Full strip of the old covering, felt and battens
  • ✓ Skip and waste removal included in the price
  • ✓ New breathable membrane, not the old sarking felt
  • ✓ Ridge, hip and valley detailing done properly, not just re-bedded
  • ✓ A walk-round with you before we leave site
02

Repairs & leaks

Most leaks are a small, findable fault — a slipped tile, failed flashing, a cracked ridge. We diagnose properly before quoting, rather than replacing whatever’s nearest.

Typical price
£220 – £900
Response
Same week, active leaks priority
Guarantee
2 years on the repair
  • ✓ A proper inspection first — no guessed quotes
  • ✓ Photos of the actual fault before any work starts
  • ✓ Like-for-like tile and slate matching where possible
  • ✓ A written note on what caused it, so it doesn’t happen again
03

Flat roofing

GRP fibreglass and single-ply systems for extensions, garages and dormers — laid by the crew that quoted it, not a subcontracted specialist we’ve never worked with.

Typical price
£2,400 – £4,800
On site
1–2 days
Guarantee
20 years, written
  • ✓ GRP fibreglass or single-ply, whichever suits the job
  • ✓ Correct falls built in, so water actually drains
  • ✓ Trims and upstands finished properly, not just sealed over
04

Guttering & flashing

The detailing that decides whether a roof actually stays dry — lead work, fascias, soffits and full guttering runs.

Typical price
£650 – £2,200
On site
1 day, most jobs
Guarantee
10 years, written
  • ✓ Code 4/5 lead to current building regs, not bitumen substitutes
  • ✓ Cast-iron effect or genuine cast-iron guttering options
  • ✓ Fascia and soffit boards replaced or over-boarded, your choice

What people ask before booking.

Usually not. Re-roofing like-for-like normally falls under permitted development rights in England, so no planning application is needed. The exceptions are listed buildings and some conservation areas, where the council may ask for matching materials or extra sign-off — we’ll flag it during the quote if your property’s affected.

Yes. Every quoted price includes scaffolding, skip hire and waste removal — the figure you’re given is what you pay, not a starting point with extras added later.

The roof is sheeted and made weathertight at the end of every working day, so a rained-off day just pushes the finish date back rather than risking the property. We’ll tell you as soon as we know, not the morning it happens.

A small deposit secures the date and materials order; the balance is due on completion, after the walk-round. Nothing is asked for upfront in full.

Workmanship on the job itself — if a covered fault shows up within the guarantee period (25 years on a re-roof, 20 on flat roofing, 10 on guttering, 2 on a repair), we come back and fix it at no cost. It’s registered in your name and sent in writing, not just said on site.
