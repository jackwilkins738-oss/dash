Build & publish a client's site

  1. Draft their site on Calls writes outreach/sites/<folder>/ with a brief, proposal and site.json.
  2. Write copy (AI) (optional) rewrites the words in site.json - headline, services, town pages, FAQs - from their answers and old site. The old file is kept as site.before-copy.json.
  3. Fill in and check site.json; anything marked [Confirm] blocks a real build (the log lists it).
  4. Show the client before it's live: tick Draft build, Build site, then Publish preview - send them the preview.<project>.pages.dev link. They tap Leave feedback and pin comments on the page; each lands in /admin and your inbox.
  5. Build site makes every page in site/; Check site runs the pre-launch QA (Publish runs it too, and won't publish with problems). Publish site puts only that folder live on their Cloudflare. Needs CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN and Node.js.

Data requests (UK GDPR)

If anyone asks what you hold on them, press Export. It writes outreach/data-requests/<date>-<website>.txt to send them; reply within a month. If they ask you to delete it, press Erase. That removes them from every list, CSV and their preview page, and keeps only their website and email on do-not-contact so no future list emails them. Delete their emails from your inbox yourself.
